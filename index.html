RHAGE study

A short research study by PLAISense on what makes games fun. It takes about 12 minutes.

What you doWatch 1-minute Plays of gameplay, tap the intense moments and tag what you feel. Answer a few short questions.
What we saveYour taps, labels and typed notes, your answers, and your screen size. Nothing else.
What we don'tNo name, email, voice or camera. Your answers are stored under an anonymous ID.
Your choiceEvery question can be skipped. Stop any time. To delete your answers, open the app again and tap “Delete my answers”.
How long we keep itUp to 24 months, for research only. Then it is deleted.
About youSome questions ask about your play style. They are optional.
