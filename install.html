PLAISENSE RHAGE

Tag your plays. Feel your moments. About 12 minutes, landscape, sound on.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open RHAGE
  3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open RHAGE from your home screen and turn the phone sideways.
It runs full screen like an app and updates itself.

Android

Download the app (40 MB)Or play in the browser
  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. On Samsung, turn Auto Blocker off for the install, then back on.
  3. Tap Install, open RHAGE, turn the phone sideways.

Play in the browser
